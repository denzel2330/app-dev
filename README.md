## **My Favorite Movie**
### Saving Private Ryan

Saving private ryan is a movie made in 1998 which was was set in WW2 nearing the end of the war
The story starts with operation of **D-Day** in 1944 where the allies first landed in germany to 
retake France. It was a breath taking and horrifying scene, depicting the brutality of war and
the experiences of the soldiers both allies and German. At the end of the battle we pan back to the
beach with bodies piled on the shore as we pan over to a soldiers backpack with the name **Ryan**

this story is the lives of a group of US soldiers lead by Cpt. John Miller played by actor Tom Hanks
as they go through Germany in finding a lost paratrooper named James Ryan to send him back home after
his other 3 brothers death.
